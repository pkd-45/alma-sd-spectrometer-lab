# Public Hector evidence and safe wording

The public `Hector-Galaxy-Survey/hector` repository identifies itself as the Hector data-reduction pipeline and includes a 22 September 2023 commit whose message states that `import_aat()` imports files from all dates of an observing run "following Pratyush's method".

Public references:

- Repository: https://github.com/Hector-Galaxy-Survey/hector
- Commit: https://github.com/Hector-Galaxy-Survey/hector/commit/414ebc4d6d38d7787416baa76eb0a1d41ef9076f

Safe application wording:

> In the Hector survey collaboration I contributed operational data-reduction workflow knowledge, including the observing-run import method adopted in the public Hector pipeline, and I use the pipeline and its cube products in survey validation and science analysis.

Do not claim authorship of that commit unless you can document that separately. The public evidence supports methodological contribution and collaboration, not necessarily commit authorship.
